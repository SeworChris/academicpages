<p>
I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the <a href="https://www.mcgill.ca/equity-ethics-policy/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Department of Equity, Ethics and Policy</a> at McGill University, Montréal, Canada. My research focuses on understanding how environmental and climate-related exposures influence cardiovascular, respiratory, and developmental health outcomes. Drawing on environmental epidemiology, exposure science, biostatistics, and public health, I investigate how air pollution, extreme weather events, wildfire smoke, freezing rain, and other environmental stressors affect human health across diverse populations.
</p>

<p>
Prior to joining McGill University, I completed a PhD in Environmental Health (Epidemiology Specialization) at <a href="https://www.colostate.edu" target="_blank"> Colorado State University (CSU)</a>, USA. My doctoral research examined how household air pollution contributes to non-communicable disease risk among vulnerable populations. Using data from both intervention and observational studies, I investigated the relationships between household air pollution, inflammation, cardiovascular health, and adverse pregnancy and birth outcomes. I was also interested in understanding how modifiable factors, including diet and physical activity, influence the health effects of environmental exposures.
</p>

<p>
My current research integrates large administrative health databases, pharmaceutical claims data, environmental monitoring systems, climate records, and geospatial datasets to evaluate exposure-response relationships and identify populations at increased environmental health risk. I apply longitudinal, spatial, and time-series epidemiologic methods to study the health impacts of air pollution and climate-related exposures. In addition, I conduct multinational research examining how extreme weather events affect child health and early childhood development in low- and middle-income countries, with a particular interest in environmental health equity and vulnerable populations.
</p>

<p>
I earned a Master of Public Health degree from <a href="https://www.cukerala.ac.in/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Central University of Kerala (CUK)</a>, India, through the prestigious African Scholarship Scheme under the Indian Council for Cultural Relations. For my master's dissertation, I analyzed Demographic and Health Surveys (DHS) and Multiple Indicator Cluster Surveys (MICS) data to examine trends in the determinants of child malnutrition in Ghana between 2003 and 2017. I received a Bachelor of Science degree in Biomedical Sciences from the <a href="https://ucc.edu.gh/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"> University of Cape Coast (UCC)</a>, Ghana.
</p>

<p>
My research interests include environmental epidemiology, climate and health, air pollution, exposure assessment, cardiovascular and respiratory health, maternal and child health, environmental justice, and global public health. I am passionate about generating evidence that informs interventions, policies, and public health strategies aimed at reducing environmental health risks and advancing health equity worldwide.
</p>

<p>
Outside of research, I enjoy spending time with my family, listening to music and audiobooks, and exploring conversations at the intersection of faith and science.
</p>
